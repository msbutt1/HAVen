Electrode placement

Forearm seen from the inner (palm-up) side, elbow on the left, wrist on the right.

IN+ and IN−Both on the muscle that bulges when you make a fist, in a line along the arm, 2–3 cm apart. IN+ about one third of the way from elbow to wrist.
REFBony point at the back of the elbow. Little muscle under it, so it stays quiet when you grip.
Steps
  1. Make a tight fist once to find the muscle. Mark the spot, then relax.
  2. Clean the three spots with alcohol or soap and water. Let them dry.
  3. Press each pad on firmly for a few seconds. Keep the cable from pulling.
  4. Rest the arm flat, relaxed, before starting any calibration.
Avoid
  • REF on muscle or on a tendon at the wrist (it adds noise).
  • IN+ and IN− across the arm instead of along it.
  • Pads resting on hair, or on skin that is still wet from cleaning.
