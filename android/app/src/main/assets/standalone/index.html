How it works

What the vibration bar means, in plain words.

Why it matters

Using shaking tools like drills, grinders and breakers for a long time, day after day, can hurt your hands and arms. It can cause tingling, numb fingers, white fingers in the cold, and a weaker grip. It builds up slowly, so it is easy to miss.

What the sleeve feels

Two things on your forearm, while you use a tool:

  • How much your arm shakes.
  • How hard you grip. A tight grip lets more shaking into your hand.

The bar only fills while you are using a tool. It fills faster when the shaking is stronger, when you grip harder, and the longer you keep going.

The colours

Low. Under half of today's guide.
Getting high. Over half. Plan breaks and other tasks.
Over today's guide. Talk to your supervisor about the rest of the day.

Today's guide is a level your team chose for this project. It is not a legal limit, and a full bar does not mean you are hurt.

Tool time

The phone knows when you are using a power tool and counts your tool time. Normal moving, like shaking hands or waving, does not count. After 20 minutes of tool use without a break, it reminds you to take one.

Simple tips

  • Take short breaks from the tool.
  • Let the tool do the work. Do not grip harder than you need to.
  • Keep your hands warm and dry.
  • Tell your supervisor if your fingers tingle, go numb or turn white.

Good to know

Each day is saved by itself and starts again at midnight. Set up the sleeve each time you put it on, so it knows your own grip.

This is a test version, not a medical device. It cannot tell you if you are injured.

Details for the team

Technical readings for the people setting up and checking the system.

Score rate (au per hour) = V² × (1 + a), V = vibration RMS above 5 Hz in m/s², a = grip effort (0 relaxed, 1 calibrated squeeze, capped at 2). Today's total adds the score only while a tool is detected. Experimental, arbitrary units: not A(8), not a dose. Default guide 75 au = 8 h at 2.5 m/s² with 50 % grip.
