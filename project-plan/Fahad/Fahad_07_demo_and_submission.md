# Demo, pitch, and delivery

## Demo sequence
1. Start the local system and confirm fresh live readings.
2. Calibrate the wearer and show the muscle-activation indicator changing with a squeeze.
3. Use a controlled vibration source to change the vibration indicator.
4. Show session recording and explain the experimental score separately.
5. Run a short perception-test preview, record responses, and save its result.
6. Show clearly labeled simulated longitudinal history.

Show instantaneous measurements and score rate rather than expecting a daily accumulation to visibly jump in seconds. Label any accelerated time. Avoid unsafe loose-grip demonstrations with running tools; use a controlled setup appropriate to the venue and equipment.

## Pitch position
“We built an integrated prototype combining wearable muscle and vibration measurements with repeatable sensory-test workflows.”

Do not claim that this build diagnoses damage, measures true transmitted dose accurately, validates causal links, or replaces professional equipment. Verify any epidemiology, pricing, competitor comparisons, and standard-limit claims before including them. Do not present illustrative thresholds as validated cutoffs.

## Release checklist
- Local startup/restart instructions and known-good firmware available.
- Live, replay, and simulated data visibly distinguished.
- Backup video plays locally; recording demonstrates actual achieved behavior.
- Spare electrodes, cables, mounting supplies, and required power supplies packed.
- Every teammate knows their speaking role and fallback transition.
- Submission assets and deadline checked; no new features after freeze.
